# Store listing: English (en-US)

## App name

```
Trace: Travel Map Videos
```

## Short description

```
Turn your Google Maps Timeline into a short video of your trips, with photos.
```

## Full description

```
Trace turns the places you've been into a short, calm video. A line crosses the map, the camera follows it, and your photos come up where you took them. Post it as a story, or keep it as a memory of the trip.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Export your Timeline from Google Maps on your phone. Trace shows you where to tap.
2. Choose the days: a weekend, a holiday, a whole year.
3. Change the look while the preview plays, then save an MP4.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Camera: lock on to the dot, glide smoothly, turn with the road like a satnav, cut between steady shots, or show the whole route
• Speed: even all the way, or true to life, so walks take their time and drives rush by
• Photos and short clips come up on the route as a card, a print, on the map or in a corner, with the place and time
• Find the photos and videos of the chosen days in your gallery in one tap
• Six map styles (three need a free CARTO key), any line colour, a title, the date and the distance
• 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9, from 6 to 60 seconds, 720p or 1080p, 30 or 60 fps
• Tap away GPS errors, and save the whole route as an image

PRIVATE
Your Timeline is read on your phone and never uploaded. No account, no ads, no tracking. Trace only goes online for map tiles, and for the names of the places under your photos if you want them.

Trace reads the Timeline export from Android and iPhone, and Records.json and Semantic Location History from Google Takeout. In English and Russian.

Trace is an independent open-source app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Google Maps is a trademark of Google LLC. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
```
